            <h2><i class="fas fa-flask"></i> Research Experience</h2>
            <div class="research-experience">
                <div class="research-project">
                    <h4><i class="fas fa-robot"></i> An LLM-Driven Simulation Framework for Environmental Human Behavior</h4>
                    <p class="collaborators">with Y. Yoo & D. Shin</p>
                    <ul class="research-achievements">
                        <li>Developed Person-Environment-Situation (P.E.S.) framework extending Lewin's behavioral theory for LLM-based spatial choice prediction</li>
                        <li><strong>Validated framework against 789K real usage records achieving 83% accuracy improvement</strong> over existing methods</li>
                        <li>Implemented multi-agent system for Cold-Start prediction where historical behavioral data is unavailable</li>
                    </ul>
                </div>

                <div class="research-project">
                    <h4><i class="fas fa-ban"></i> Regulating AI-Generated Content: When Do Restrictions Spark or Stifle Community Dynamics</h4>
                    <p class="collaborators">with J. Shin, D. Shin, & W. Oh</p>
                    <ul class="research-achievements">
                        <li><strong>Examined AI content bans across 743 Reddit communities</strong> using difference-in-differences methodology</li>
                        <li>Found opposing effects by community type: decreased participation in information-focused vs increased engagement in creativity-focused communities</li>
                        <li>Established that AI restrictions function as authenticity signals in creative spaces</li>
                    </ul>
                </div>

                <div class="research-project">
                    <h4><i class="fas fa-align-left"></i> Asymmetric AI: How Text-Only Summarization Reshapes Creator Behavior on Digital Platforms</h4>
                    <p class="collaborators">with D. Shin</p>
                    <ul class="research-achievements">
                        <li>Investigating creator adaptation to text-only AI summarization using Google Maps and Amazon platforms</li>
                        <li>Analyzing how asymmetric AI processing influences multimodal content creation strategies</li>
                    </ul>
                </div>
            </div>
