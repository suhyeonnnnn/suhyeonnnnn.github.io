    <!-- Abstract -->
    <section class="section fade-in-delay">
        <h2 class="section-title">Abstract</h2>
        <div class="section-content">
            <p>
                This research examines the unintended consequences of gaming regulations during the COVID-19 pandemic. By analyzing 
                how lockdown measures and gaming time restrictions affected online game consumption patterns, we investigate whether 
                regulatory interventions achieved their intended public health objectives or created unexpected behavioral adaptations 
                among gaming communities.
            </p>
        </div>
    </section>

    <!-- Key Findings -->
    <section class="section fade-in-delay">
        <h2 class="section-title">Key Findings</h2>
        <div class="section-content">
            <div class="findings-grid">
                <div class="finding-card">
                    <div class="finding-icon">⏰</div>
                    <h4>Time Displacement</h4>
                    <p>Gaming time restrictions led to concentrated usage patterns rather than overall reduction in consumption.</p>
                </div>

                <div class="finding-card">
                    <div class="finding-icon">🔄</div>
                    <h4>Platform Switching</h4>
                    <p>Users migrated to less regulated platforms and gaming formats, undermining policy effectiveness.</p>
                </div>

                <div class="finding-card">
                    <div class="finding-icon">📱</div>
                    <h4>Mobile Gaming Surge</h4>
                    <p>COVID-19 lockdowns increased mobile gaming by 67%, with differential regulatory impacts across platforms.</p>
                </div>

                <div class="finding-card">
                    <div class="finding-icon">🎯</div>
                    <h4>Unintended Consequences</h4>
                    <p>Regulations designed to protect mental health had mixed effects, sometimes increasing problematic usage patterns.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
        </div>
    </section>
