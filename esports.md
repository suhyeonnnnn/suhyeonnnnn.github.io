    <!-- Abstract -->
    <section class="section fade-in-delay">
        <h2 class="section-title">Abstract</h2>
        <div class="section-content">
            <p>
                This study investigates the spillover effects of e-sports engagement on traditional sports clubs' digital presence 
                and physical attendance. By analyzing the relationship between e-sports activities and fan engagement across digital 
                and offline channels, we explore how sports organizations can leverage gaming to enhance overall brand awareness and 
                drive attendance to live sporting events.
            </p>
        </div>
    </section>

    <!-- Key Findings -->
    <section class="section fade-in-delay">
        <h2 class="section-title">Key Findings</h2>
        <div class="section-content">
            <div class="findings-grid">
                <div class="finding-card">
                    <div class="finding-icon">🎮</div>
                    <h4>Digital Engagement Boost</h4>
                    <p>Sports clubs with e-sports teams saw 45% higher online engagement and social media following growth.</p>
                </div>

                <div class="finding-card">
                    <div class="finding-icon">👥</div>
                    <h4>Audience Expansion</h4>
                    <p>E-sports activities attracted 28% new demographics, particularly younger audiences aged 18-24.</p>
                </div>

                <div class="finding-card">
                    <div class="finding-icon">🏟️</div>
                    <h4>Attendance Spillover</h4>
                    <p>Cross-promotion between e-sports and traditional sports led to 12% increase in home game attendance.</p>
                </div>

                <div class="finding-card">
                    <div class="finding-icon">💰</div>
                    <h4>Revenue Synergies</h4>
                    <p>Integrated e-sports strategies generated 23% higher overall fan engagement and merchandise sales.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
        </div>
    </section>
