            <div class="research-subtitle">A Case Study of Changwon-si</div>

    <!-- Abstract -->
    <section class="section fade-in-delay">
        <h2 class="section-title">Abstract</h2>
        <div class="section-content">
            <p>
                This study presents a machine learning-based approach to determine optimal locations for children's smart pedestrian 
                crosswalks in urban environments. Using Changwon-si as a case study, we developed a comprehensive modeling framework 
                that integrates multiple data sources including traffic patterns, demographic information, school locations, and safety 
                incidents to identify priority areas for smart infrastructure deployment.
            </p>
        </div>
    </section>

    <!-- Research Motivation -->
    <section class="section fade-in-delay">
        <h2 class="section-title">Research Motivation</h2>
        <div class="section-content">
            <div class="motivation-card">
                <h4>🚨 Critical Safety Issue</h4>
                <p>
                    Children's pedestrian safety remains a significant concern in urban areas. Traditional approaches to crosswalk 
                    placement often rely on manual assessment and limited data, resulting in:
                </p>
                <ul>
                    <li>Suboptimal location decisions based on incomplete information</li>
                    <li>Reactive rather than proactive safety infrastructure deployment</li>
                    <li>Limited consideration of children's specific mobility patterns</li>
                </ul>
            </div>

            <div class="motivation-card">
                <h4>💡 Smart Infrastructure Opportunity</h4>
                <p>
                    Smart pedestrian crosswalks equipped with sensors and AI technology can significantly improve safety, but their 
                    effectiveness depends heavily on strategic placement:
                </p>
                <ul>
                    <li>High installation and maintenance costs require careful location selection</li>
                    <li>Need for data-driven approaches to maximize safety impact</li>
                    <li>Opportunity to integrate multiple urban data sources for informed decision-making</li>
                </ul>
            </div>
        </div>
    </section>

    <!-- Methodology -->
    <section class="section fade-in-delay">
        <h2 class="section-title">Methodology</h2>
        <div class="section-content">
            <div class="method-card">
                <h4>📊 Data Collection & Integration</h4>
                <p>Comprehensive multi-source data integration approach:</p>
                <ul>
                    <li><strong>Traffic Flow Data:</strong> Vehicle and pedestrian traffic patterns across the city</li>
                    <li><strong>Demographic Information:</strong> Population density and age distribution by district</li>
                    <li><strong>Educational Facilities:</strong> Location and enrollment data for schools and kindergartens</li>
                    <li><strong>Safety Records:</strong> Historical pedestrian accident data and incident reports</li>
                    <li><strong>Infrastructure Data:</strong> Existing crosswalk locations and traffic signal systems</li>
                </ul>
            </div>

            <div class="method-card">
                <h4>🤖 Machine Learning Framework</h4>
                <ul>
                    <li><strong>Feature Engineering:</strong> Creation of risk indicators and accessibility metrics</li>
                    <li><strong>Spatial Analysis:</strong> GIS-based processing for geographic relationship modeling</li>
                    <li><strong>Predictive Modeling:</strong> Risk assessment models for different location candidates</li>
                    <li><strong>Optimization Algorithm:</strong> Multi-objective optimization for location selection</li>
                    <li><strong>Validation Framework:</strong> Cross-validation with expert assessments and safety outcomes</li>
                </ul>
            </div>

            <div class="method-card">
                <h4>🎯 Evaluation Metrics</h4>
                <ul>
                    <li><strong>Safety Impact Score:</strong> Predicted reduction in pedestrian incidents</li>
                    <li><strong>Accessibility Index:</strong> Proximity to schools and residential areas</li>
                    <li><strong>Traffic Integration:</strong> Compatibility with existing traffic management systems</li>
                    <li><strong>Cost-Benefit Ratio:</strong> Economic efficiency of deployment</li>
                </ul>
            </div>
        </div>
    </section>

    <!-- Key Findings -->
    <section class="section fade-in-delay">
        <h2 class="section-title">Key Findings</h2>
        <div class="section-content">

                <div class="finding-card">
                    <div class="finding-icon">📈</div>
                    <h4>Risk Prediction Accuracy</h4>

                </div>

                <div class="finding-card">
                    <div class="finding-icon">💰</div>
                    <h4>Cost Optimization</h4>

                </div>

                <div class="finding-card">
                    <div class="finding-icon">🗺️</div>
                    <h4>Spatial Patterns</h4>

                </div>
            </div>

    </section>
