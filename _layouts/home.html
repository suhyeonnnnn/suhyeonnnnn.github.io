I'm a Ph.D. student in Management Engineering at KAIST, advised by Prof. Donghyuk Shin. I study AI-mediated markets: what changes when AI systems come to sit between people and the choices they make. I look at this from three sides, asking whether marketing still works once an agent does the buying, whether platform rules still govern as intended once AI generates and filters content, and how these systems should be built to work with people in the first place. Before starting my Ph.D., I was a visiting student at Carnegie Mellon University's AI Intensive Program.
