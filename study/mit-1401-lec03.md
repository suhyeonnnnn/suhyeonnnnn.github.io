    <!-- 1. Budget Constraint -->
    <section class="section fade-in-delay">
        <h2 class="section-title">1. Budget Constraint</h2>
        <div class="section-content">
            
            <h4>Key Assumption: No Saving or Borrowing</h4>
            <div style="background: #fef3c7; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; margin: 1rem 0;">
                <p style="font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.1rem; text-align: center;">
                    Budget = Income
                </p>
                <p>You spend <strong>all</strong> your income on consumption goods. No saving, no borrowing.</p>
                <p><em>"Sadly, this is not a terrible approximation for Americans. The average American has less than $1,000 in available savings to access."</em></p>
            </div>

            <h4>Budget Constraint Equation</h4>
            <div style="background: #eff6ff; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; margin: 0.5rem 0;">
                <p style="font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.1rem; text-align: center;">
                    Y = P<sub>C</sub> × C + P<sub>S</sub> × S
                </p>
            </div>
            
            <table style="width:100%; border-collapse: collapse; margin: 1rem 0;">
                <tr style="border-bottom: 1px solid #e5e7eb;">
                    <td style="padding: 0.5rem; font-weight: 600; width: 20%;">Y</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.5rem;">Your income (given)</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="border-bottom: 1px solid #e5e7eb;">
                    <td style="padding: 0.5rem; font-weight: 600;">P<sub>C</sub> × C</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.5rem;">Total spending on cookies = (price per cookie) × (number of cookies)</td>
                </tr>
                <tr>
                    <td style="padding: 0.5rem; font-weight: 600;">P<sub>S</sub> × S</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.5rem;">Total spending on pizza = (price per slice) × (number of slices)</td>
                </tr>
            </table>

            <h4>Numerical Example</h4>
            <table style="width:100%; border-collapse: collapse; margin: 1rem 0;">
                <tr style="background: #f8fafc; border-bottom: 2px solid #e5e7eb;">
                    <th style="padding: 0.75rem; text-align: left;">Parameter</th>
                    <th style="padding: 0.75rem; text-align: left;">Value</th>
                </tr>
                <tr style="border-bottom: 1px solid #e5e7eb;">
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Income (Y)</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">$24</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="border-bottom: 1px solid #e5e7eb;">
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Price of cookies (P<sub>C</sub>)</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">$2</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="border-bottom: 1px solid #e5e7eb;">
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Price of pizza slice (P<sub>S</sub>)</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">$4</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="border-bottom: 1px solid #e5e7eb;">
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">X-intercept (all cookies)</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Y/P<sub>C</sub> = 24/2 = 12 cookies</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="border-bottom: 1px solid #e5e7eb;">
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Y-intercept (all pizza)</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Y/P<sub>S</sub> = 24/4 = 6 slices</td>
                </tr>
                <tr>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Slope</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">−P<sub>C</sub>/P<sub>S</sub> = −2/4 = −1/2</td>
                </tr>
            </table>

            <h4>The Opportunity Set</h4>
            <div style="background: #f0fdf4; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; margin: 1rem 0;">
                <p><strong>Definition:</strong> The area <strong>under</strong> the budget constraint represents all affordable combinations.</p>
                <p><strong>Q:</strong> "If we assume all income is spent, why look at the area below the line?"</p>
                <p><strong>A:</strong> You <em>will</em> always be on the line. But the opportunity set matters because when it shrinks, you have fewer choices available → you're worse off.</p>
            </div>
        </div>
    </section>

    <!-- 2. Marginal Rate of Transformation -->
    <section class="section fade-in-delay">
        <h2 class="section-title">2. Marginal Rate of Transformation (MRT)</h2>
        <div class="section-content">
            
            <h4>Definition</h4>
            <div style="background: #eff6ff; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; margin: 0.5rem 0;">
                <p style="font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.1rem; text-align: center;">
                    MRT = −P<sub>C</sub>/P<sub>S</sub> = Slope of the budget constraint
                </p>
            </div>
            
            <p>The rate at which you can <strong>transform</strong> one good into another <strong>through the market</strong>.</p>

            <div style="background: #fef3c7; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; margin: 1rem 0;">
                <p><em>"This is not a course about alchemy. We're not going to teach you how to transform cookies into pizza."</em></p>
                <p style="margin-top: 0.5rem;">But given prices and income, <strong>opportunity cost</strong> means:</p>
                <ul>
                    <li>Every cookie you buy = 1/2 slice of pizza you <strong>can't</strong> buy</li>
                    <li>Every pizza slice you buy = 2 cookies you <strong>can't</strong> buy</li>
                </ul>
            </div>

            <h4>MRS vs MRT: The Critical Distinction</h4>
            <table style="width:100%; border-collapse: collapse; margin: 1rem 0;">
                <tr style="background: #f8fafc; border-bottom: 2px solid #e5e7eb;">
                    <th style="padding: 0.75rem; text-align: left;">Concept</th>
                    <th style="padding: 0.75rem; text-align: left;">Formula</th>
                    <th style="padding: 0.75rem; text-align: left;">What It Represents</th>
                    <th style="padding: 0.75rem; text-align: left;">Determined By</th>
                </tr>
                <tr style="border-bottom: 1px solid #e5e7eb;">
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem; font-weight: 600;">MRS</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">−MU<sub>C</sub>/MU<sub>S</sub></td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Rate you're <strong>willing</strong> to trade</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Your preferences (internal)</td>
                </tr>
                <tr>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem; font-weight: 600;">MRT</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">−P<sub>C</sub>/P<sub>S</sub></td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Rate you <strong>can</strong> trade</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Market prices (external)</td>
                </tr>
            </table>
        </div>
    </section>

    <!-- 4. Changes in Budget Constraint -->
    <section class="section fade-in-delay">
        <h2 class="section-title">4. Changes in the Budget Constraint</h2>
        <div class="section-content">
            
            <h4>Two Ways the Budget Constraint Can Change</h4>
            <table style="width:100%; border-collapse: collapse; margin: 1rem 0;">
                <tr style="background: #f8fafc; border-bottom: 2px solid #e5e7eb;">
                    <th style="padding: 0.75rem; text-align: left;">Change</th>
                    <th style="padding: 0.75rem; text-align: left;">Effect</th>
                    <th style="padding: 0.75rem; text-align: left;">Slope</th>
                </tr>
                <tr style="border-bottom: 1px solid #e5e7eb;">
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Price change</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Budget line <strong>pivots</strong></td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Changes</td>
                </tr>
                <tr>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Income change</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Budget line <strong>shifts parallel</strong></td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Unchanged</td>
                </tr>
            </table>

            <!-- Case 1: Price Change -->
            <div style="background: #f8fafc; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; margin: 1rem 0; border-left: 4px solid #ef4444;">
                <p style="font-weight: 600; color: #dc2626;">Case 1: Price Change (Pizza $4 → $6)</p>
                <ul>
                    <li>X-intercept (cookies): <strong>Unchanged</strong> at 12</li>
                    <li>Y-intercept (pizza): <strong>Decreased</strong> from 6 to 4</li>
                    <li>New slope: −2/6 = <strong>−1/3</strong> (was −1/2)</li>
                    <li>Opportunity set shrinks → You are worse off</li>
                </ul>
                <p><em>"Your income hasn't changed. You've still got 24 bucks. But you're worse off because your income is just a representation of what you can buy."</em></p>
            </div>

            <!-- Case 2: Income Change -->
            <div style="background: #f8fafc; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; margin: 1rem 0; border-left: 4px solid #f59e0b;">
                <p style="font-weight: 600; color: #d97706;">Case 2: Income Change ($24 → $20)</p>
                <ul>
                    <li>Slope: <strong>Unchanged</strong> at −1/2 (prices didn't change)</li>
                    <li>Both intercepts decrease</li>
                    <li>Opportunity set shrinks → You are worse off</li>
                </ul>
            </div>

            <h4>What About Inflation?</h4>
            <div style="background: #f0f9ff; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; margin: 1rem 0;">
                <ul>
                    <li><strong>Uniform inflation</strong> (all prices rise by same %): Looks like income decrease (parallel shift)</li>
                    <li><strong>Differential inflation</strong> (prices rise by different %): Looks like price change (pivot)</li>
                </ul>
            </div>
        </div>
    </section>

    <!-- 5. Constrained Choice -->
    <section class="section fade-in-delay">
        <h2 class="section-title">5. Constrained Choice: Finding the Optimum</h2>
        <div class="section-content">
            
            <h4>The Core Problem</h4>
            <div style="background: #f0f9ff; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; margin: 1rem 0;">
                <ul>
                    <li><strong>Lecture 2:</strong> More is better (reach highest IC possible)</li>
                    <li><strong>Lecture 3:</strong> You can't consume more than your budget allows</li>
                </ul>
                <p><strong>Solution:</strong> Choose the <strong>highest indifference curve</strong> that still <strong>touches the budget constraint</strong>.</p>
            </div>

            <h4>Mathematical Condition: MRS = MRT</h4>
            <div style="background: #eff6ff; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; margin: 0.5rem 0;">
                <p>At tangency, the slopes are equal:</p>
                <p style="font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.1rem; text-align: center;">
                    MRS = MRT
                </p>
                <p style="font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.1rem; text-align: center;">
                    −MU<sub>C</sub>/MU<sub>S</sub> = −P<sub>C</sub>/P<sub>S</sub>
                </p>
                <p style="text-align: center;">Or equivalently:</p>
                <p style="font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.1rem; text-align: center;">
                    <strong>MU<sub>C</sub>/MU<sub>S</sub> = P<sub>C</sub>/P<sub>S</sub></strong>
                </p>
            </div>

            <h4>The "Bang for the Buck" Interpretation</h4>
            <div style="background: #fef3c7; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; margin: 1rem 0;">
                <p>Rearranging the optimization condition:</p>
                <p style="font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.1rem; text-align: center;">
                    MU<sub>C</sub>/P<sub>C</sub> = MU<sub>S</sub>/P<sub>S</sub>
                </p>
                <ul>
                    <li><strong>Bang</strong> = MU (happiness from next unit)</li>
                    <li><strong>Buck</strong> = P (cost of next unit)</li>
                    <li><strong>Bang per Buck</strong> = MU/P (happiness per dollar)</li>
                </ul>
                <p><strong>Rule:</strong> Consume until the happiness-per-dollar is equal for all goods.</p>
            </div>

            <h4>Graphical Comparison of Points</h4>
            <table style="width:100%; border-collapse: collapse; margin: 1rem 0;">
                <tr style="background: #f8fafc; border-bottom: 2px solid #e5e7eb;">
                    <th style="padding: 0.75rem; text-align: left;">Point</th>
                    <th style="padding: 0.75rem; text-align: left;">Location</th>
                    <th style="padding: 0.75rem; text-align: left;">Utility (U = √(S×C))</th>
                    <th style="padding: 0.75rem; text-align: left;">Why Not Optimal?</th>
                </tr>
                <tr style="border-bottom: 1px solid #e5e7eb;">
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">A</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">On budget line</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">√10 ≈ 3.16</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Lower IC than D</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="border-bottom: 1px solid #e5e7eb;">
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem; font-weight: 600; color: #10b981;">D</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Tangent to budget line</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem; font-weight: 600;">√18 ≈ 4.24</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem; color: #10b981;"><strong>Optimal!</strong></td>
                </tr>
                <tr>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">E</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Beyond budget line</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">√32 ≈ 5.66</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Can't afford it</td>
                </tr>
            </table>
        </div>

    <!-- 6. Mathematical Example -->
    <section class="section fade-in-delay">
        <h2 class="section-title">6. Why Point A Is Not Optimal: Mathematical Proof</h2>
        <div class="section-content">
            
            <h4>Setup</h4>
            <ul>
                <li>U = √(S × C), Point A: S = 5, C = 2</li>
                <li>P<sub>C</sub> = $2, P<sub>S</sub> = $4</li>
            </ul>

            <h4>Step 1: Calculate MRS at Point A</h4>
            <div style="background: #f5f5f5; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; margin: 0.5rem 0;">
                <p style="font-family: monospace;">
                    MU<sub>C</sub> = 0.5 × 5 / √10 = 2.5 / √10<br>
                    MU<sub>S</sub> = 0.5 × 2 / √10 = 1 / √10<br><br>
                    MRS = −MU<sub>C</sub>/MU<sub>S</sub> = −2.5/1 = <strong>−2.5</strong>
                </p>
            </div>

            <h4>Step 2: Calculate MRT</h4>
            <div style="background: #f5f5f5; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; margin: 0.5rem 0;">
                <p style="font-family: monospace;">
                    MRT = −P<sub>C</sub>/P<sub>S</sub> = −2/4 = <strong>−0.5</strong>
                </p>
            </div>

            <h4>Step 3: Interpret the Disequilibrium</h4>
            <div style="background: #fef2f2; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; margin: 1rem 0;">
                <table style="width:100%; border-collapse: collapse;">
                    <tr style="border-bottom: 1px solid #e5e7eb;">
                        <td style="padding: 0.75rem;"><strong>MRS = −2.5</strong></td>
                        <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">You're <strong>willing</strong> to give up 2.5 pizzas for 1 cookie</td>
                    </tr>
                    <tr>
                        <td style="padding: 0.75rem;"><strong>MRT = −0.5</strong></td>
                        <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Market only <strong>requires</strong> 0.5 pizza for 1 cookie</td>
                    </tr>
                </table>
                <p style="margin-top: 1rem;"><strong>Conclusion:</strong> You value cookies highly but the market says they're cheap! Great deal → Buy more cookies!</p>
            </div>

            <h4>General Rule</h4>
            <table style="width:100%; border-collapse: collapse; margin: 1rem 0;">
                <tr style="background: #f8fafc; border-bottom: 2px solid #e5e7eb;">
                    <th style="padding: 0.75rem; text-align: left;">Condition</th>
                    <th style="padding: 0.75rem; text-align: left;">Action</th>
                </tr>
                <tr style="border-bottom: 1px solid #e5e7eb;">
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">|MRS| > |MRT|</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Buy more of x-axis good (cookies)</td>
                </tr>
                <tr>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">|MRS| < |MRT|</td>
                    <td style="padding: 0.75rem;">Buy more of y-axis good (pizza)</td>
                </tr>
            </table>
        </div>

    <section class="section fade-in-delay">
        <h2 class="section-title">7. Policy Application: SNAP vs Cash Transfers</h2>

                    <li>Eligibility: Below or near poverty line (~$14,000/year)</li>

                <p><strong>Question:</strong> Why not just give people cash?</p>
            </div>

            <h4>Two Types of People</h4>

            <h4>Effect of $500 SNAP (Food Only)</h4>

            <div style="background: #fef2f2; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; margin: 1rem 0;">
                <p><strong>Person X:</strong> Forced to change behavior!</p>
                <ul>
                    <li>Wanted (with cash): $5,200 shelter, $300 food</li>
                    <li>Gets (with SNAP): $5,000 shelter, $500 food</li>
                </ul>
                <p><strong>Lower indifference curve → Worse off!</strong></p>
            </div>

            <h4>Why Do Policymakers Use SNAP?</h4>
            <div style="background: #fef3c7; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; margin: 1rem 0;">
                <p><em>"Cross out the word 'shelter' and put in the word 'cocaine'."</em></p>
                <p>Policymakers worry: "What if poor people spend cash on drugs instead of food?"</p>
            </div>

            <h4>Empirical Evidence (J-PAL Experiments)</h4>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Positive:</strong> SNAP does change behavior — $1 in SNAP → $0.15 more food than cash</li>
                <li><strong>Normative:</strong> Cash recipients invest in education, businesses, health — rarely on "bad" things</li>
                <li><strong>Uganda:</strong> $150 cash → doubled earnings in 18 months</li>
            </ul>
        </div>
