        <div class="section-content">
            <div class="news-item">
                <div class="news-date">Current</div>
                <div class="news-title">
                    When the Buyer Is a Machine: The Diagnosticity-Exploration Tradeoff in Agentic Commerce
                    <a href="agentic-commerce.html" class="read-more">Read more</a>
                </div>
                <div class="news-description">
                    <em>with D. Shin & S. P. Han</em> &middot; In preparation for <em>Information Systems Research</em><br>
                    • Built a sandboxed e-commerce environment and collected roughly 23,000 agent purchases across commercial LLM agents, with attribute-matched fictional brands<br>
                    • Found that agents open only one or two of eight candidates before deciding, and that aggregated social proof moves them while scarcity and urgency do not<br>
                    • Accepted for presentation at ICIS 2026 (Lisbon) and INFORMS CIST 2026 (San Francisco)
                </div>
            </div>

            <div class="news-item">
                <div class="news-date">Current</div>
                <div class="news-title">
                    Regulating AI-Generated Content: When Do Restrictions Spark or Stifle Community Dynamics
                    <a href="ai-content-regulation.html" class="read-more">Read more</a>
                </div>
                <div class="news-description">
                    <em>with J. Shin, D. Shin, & W. Oh</em> &middot; In preparation for <em>MIS Quarterly</em><br>
                    • Examined AI content bans across 743 Reddit communities using staggered difference-in-differences<br>
                    • Found opposing effects by community type: participation fell in information communities and rose in creative ones<br>
                    • Best Student Paper Award, KMIS 2025; Best Student Paper Nominee, WISE 2025
                </div>
            </div>

            <div class="news-item">
                <div class="news-date">Current</div>
                <div class="news-title">
                    The Summarization Paradox: AI Summarization and Creator Multimodal Content Strategies
                    <a href="ai-summarization.html" class="read-more">Read more</a>
                </div>
                <div class="news-description">
                    <em>with S. Kim & D. Shin</em> &middot; In preparation for <em>Journal of the Association for Information Systems</em><br>
                    • Studying how creators adapted after Google Maps introduced Gemini-based review summaries<br>
                    • Because the summarizer reads text but not images, creators posted fewer images and left rating-only reviews<br>
                    • Presented at the KrAIS Summer Workshop, 2026
                </div>
            </div>

            <div class="news-item">
                <div class="news-date">Current</div>
                <div class="news-title">
                    Predicting Spatial Choice Without Historical Data: A Theory-Guided LLM Simulation Framework
                    <a href="llm-simulation.html" class="read-more">Read more</a>
                </div>
                <div class="news-description">
                    <em>with Y. Yu & D. Shin</em> &middot; In preparation for <em>Journal of Management Information Systems</em><br>
                    • Developed the Person-Environment-Situation (P.E.S.) framework so that behavioral theory, not history, constrains LLM-based prediction<br>
                    • Validated against 1.1M real check-in records with the later period held out, addressing the cold-start setting where no training data exists<br>
                    • Earlier version published at CIKM 2025
                </div>
            </div>
        </div>
